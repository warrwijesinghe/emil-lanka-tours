The terms “private driver” and “chauffeur guide” are sometimes used interchangeably, but travellers may expect different things from each. The useful question is not which label is better; it is what support will make your own Sri Lanka itinerary easier and more enjoyable.

## What a private driver does

A private driver provides dedicated transport for your route. They collect you, take you between hotels and destinations, help keep travel days practical and allow stops that fit the journey. This can be especially convenient when travelling through several regions, carrying luggage, or travelling as a family or small group.

For a straightforward route with confirmed hotels and activities, a private driver may be exactly what you need.

## What a chauffeur guide adds

A chauffeur guide combines driving with more hands-on travel assistance. Depending on the journey, that can include local context, help with route decisions, suggestions for breaks and support in keeping a multi-stop itinerary running smoothly. It is not a substitute for a specialist guide where one is required, but it can make independent travel feel more connected.

## Transport versus travel assistance

Think about the rhythm of your trip. If you want to reach each hotel efficiently, transport may be the priority. If you would welcome informed suggestions, flexible stops and a local point of contact as plans evolve, chauffeur-guided support can offer more value.

## When each option suits travellers

### A private driver may be enough when

- Your itinerary is simple and your accommodation is already arranged.
- You mainly need reliable transfers between a small number of places.
- You prefer to organise visits independently.

### A chauffeur-guided journey may suit you when

- You are travelling between several destinations such as [Sigiriya](/destinations/sigiriya/), Kandy, Ella and the coast.
- You would like flexibility for scenic stops, meal breaks and route adjustments.
- You are a couple, family or small group looking for a more supported journey.

## Questions to ask before booking

Ask who will be with you during the trip, what is included beyond driving, the vehicle proposed for your luggage, and how route changes are handled. Also ask whether local site guides should be arranged separately for places where specialist interpretation matters.
