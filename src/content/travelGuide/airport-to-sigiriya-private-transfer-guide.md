Sigiriya is a popular first stop for travellers heading into Sri Lanka's Cultural Triangle. After arriving at Bandaranaike International Airport, the important choice is whether to travel onward the same day or rest first near the airport.

## Think about your arrival, not only the map

International flights, immigration, luggage collection and tired children can all change how a journey feels. Arrival conditions and road conditions vary, so avoid building a tightly timed plan around assumptions. Share your flight details and hotel plan with your driver so the collection and onward journey can be planned sensibly.

## Travel onward or stay in Negombo?

For an early arrival and travellers who feel ready to continue, travelling onward to Sigiriya can make sense. A private transfer allows comfort and meal stops along the way. For late arrivals, long-haul flights or families who need time to reset, a first night in Negombo is often the calmer option before travelling inland the next day.

## What makes a private transfer useful

A pre-arranged private transfer gives you a clear meeting plan, space for luggage and the ability to stop when needed. This can be especially helpful with children, large bags or a changing flight schedule. Ask in advance about the vehicle proposed for your group and luggage, and confirm how arrival delays are handled.

## Rest stops and travelling comfortably

Plan for water, snacks, a meal break and restrooms rather than treating the journey as a race. If you are travelling with children, keep essential items accessible rather than packing them beneath all luggage. A driver can help make sensible stops, but telling them your needs at the start is always useful.
